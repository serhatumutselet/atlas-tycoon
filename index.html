Atlas
Tycoon

Buy cities on a floating island, raise landmarks and bankrupt your rivals.

How to play
  • Roll two dice and move. Doubles roll again; three doubles strand you on Castaway Isle.
  • Buy an empty city and build on it right away. First lap: up to a Villa. Second lap: a Tower. After that: a Hotel.
  • Land on your own Hotel to raise a Landmark. Landmarks can never be bought out.
  • Pay rent on a rival's city, then you may buy it out for twice its value.
  • A whole region doubles its rent. The Grand Gala doubles one property's rent again.
  • Airship Dock: pay $50 to fly anywhere. Landing exactly on Departure gives a free upgrade.
  • Win with three full regions, every property on one side, all four resorts, or by being the last one standing.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom the board, drag to move it, double-click to reset. Tap any tile for its details.

Play on this device

Pass the device around, or fill seats with bots.
